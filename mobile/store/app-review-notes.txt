therounders is a sports research app with on-device simulated paper tickets. It does not accept wagers, process payments, or connect to a sportsbook account.

No login or demo account is required. Paper tickets stay on the device and can be marked won, lost, push, or pending by the tester.

Market prices come from https://therounders.netlify.app. The current production feed is the controlled demo provider. The app labels those prices SIMULATED and keeps a PAPER ONLY badge on screen. Do not review them as live sportsbook odds.

Matchup ratings are model estimates. The screen names the training source. They are not betting advice or a guarantee.

The app uses only standard HTTPS. Export compliance: ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false.
